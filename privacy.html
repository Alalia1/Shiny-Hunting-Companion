Privacy Policy

Shiny Hunting Companion — Android app

Effective 31 August 2026 · Last updated 31 August 2026

The short version. Shiny Hunting Companion has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. Everything you record in it — your counters, your caught data, your catch history — is stored on your own device and is never sent to the developer. The developer receives no data about you of any kind.

The app does include Google AdMob advertising and Google Play in-app purchases. Those are the only components that transmit anything, and what they collect is described in full below.

1. Who this policy is from

This policy covers the Android application Shiny Hunting Companion (package io.github.alalia1.shinycompanion), referred to below as "the app". The app is an unofficial, fan-made tool for tracking shiny Pokémon hunts, and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., The Pokémon Company, or Niantic.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in its own private storage, so it can do its job:

  • Your counters, their settings, groups, categories, and hunt history
  • Your caught data across the General, generational, and per-game Pokédexes
  • Your catch records, including dates, counts, and Poké Ball selections
  • Your app preferences — theme, audio and haptic settings, and similar
  • Whether you have purchased ad removal

None of this is transmitted anywhere. The app contains no networking code of its own — it makes no requests to any server operated by the developer, because no such server exists.

3. Camera and screen capture

The app's optional Auto-Detect feature can watch a region of your screen, or use your device's camera to watch a game running on another screen, in order to count encounters automatically.

When you turn this on:

  • Frames are analysed in memory only, to compare colours within the region you selected.
  • Frames are never written to storage — no photo, video, or screenshot is saved at any point.
  • Frames are never transmitted off your device.
  • Capture runs only while you have explicitly started it, and stops when you stop it.

The feature is entirely optional and off by default. You can decline or later revoke the camera permission in Android's system settings, and the rest of the app is unaffected.

4. Advertising

The app may display banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Ads appear in at most two places — beneath the counter list and beneath the Pokédex — and never on the counter screen or the floating counter bubble. There are no interstitial, rewarded, or app-open ads.

When ads are active, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier)
  • Your IP address, and coarse location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Interactions with the ads themselves, such as views and taps

This data is collected by Google, not by the developer, and is governed by Google's own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

If you are in a region where consent is legally required, the app presents Google's User Messaging Platform consent form before any ad is requested, and you may choose whether your data is used for personalised advertising. You can revisit that choice at any time via Settings → Remove Ads / Tip Jar → Ad privacy options.

Opting out

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's system settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads on most devices. You can also remove advertising from the app entirely by purchasing the one-time Remove Ads option, after which no ad is ever requested.

5. Purchases

The app offers a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase and optional tips, handled entirely by Google Play Billing.

  • All payment processing is performed by Google. The app never sees, receives, or stores your payment details — no card number, no billing address, nothing.
  • The app receives only what Google reports about the purchase itself: which product was bought and whether it is currently owned.
  • Your ad-free status is cached on your device and re-checked with Google Play when the app resumes.

Purchases are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play's terms.

6. Backups and data you export yourself

Your data can leave your device in exactly two ways, both of which you control:

Android Auto Backup

If Android's backup service is enabled on your device, the app's saved data is included in your device backup and stored in your own Google account. This is a function of Android, not of the app; the developer has no access to it. You can disable it in your device's backup settings.

Backup & Restore

The app's Settings → Backup & Restore screen lets you export your data to a JSON file. You choose where that file is written using Android's system file picker, and the file goes only where you put it. The developer never receives it.

7. Data the developer receives

None. There is no server, no account system, no analytics, and no crash reporting in this app. The developer cannot see your hunts, your settings, your device, or whether you use the app at all.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, this is largely academic — but if you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please get in touch using the details below.

9. Your choices and controls

  • Delete everything: Settings → Reset All Data erases the app's stored data on your device. Uninstalling the app also removes it.
  • Camera: grant or revoke the permission in Android's system settings at any time.
  • Advertising: reset your advertising ID in system settings, adjust your consent choice in the app, or remove ads by purchase.
  • Backups: disable Android Auto Backup in your device settings if you do not want your app data included.

10. Data retention

Because your data is stored only on your device, retention is entirely in your hands. It persists until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall. The developer holds no copy and therefore has nothing to retain or delete on your behalf.
